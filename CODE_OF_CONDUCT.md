# Code of Conduct

LinuxPlayer is a welcoming project for people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Help others participate without gatekeeping.
- Respect privacy and copyright when sharing logs, screenshots, or sample media.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, threats, and repeated disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful comments, close threads, and restrict participation to protect the community. For public repository content, use GitHub's **Report content** action. For a private conduct report, contact the repository owner through the contact method listed on the project's GitHub profile. Maintainers will limit sharing of reports to people needed to address them and aim to acknowledge a report within seven days.
